# Glossary (English)

- **Accepted deviation:** Approved, value-specific, time-bounded exception; raw difference remains visible.
- **Alignment:** Deterministic relation between configured and selected pack value.
- **Assignment:** Intune targeting of users/devices/groups, optionally filtered/excluded.
- **Baseline:** Versioned collection of recommendations for a named product/context; not universal organizational truth.
- **Canonical setting ID:** Stable exact technical identifier used for matching.
- **Conflict confidence:** Confirmed, probable, or possible overlap after value and assignment analysis.
- **Evidence confidence:** Strength of the source/mapping; separate from extraction confidence.
- **Knowledge pack:** Hash-bound versioned settings/evidence bundle.
- **Not evaluable:** Required evidence/fact is missing; no positive or negative judgment.
- **Policy intent:** Desired exported configuration; not proof of device deployment.
- **Runtime evidence:** Deployment status/counts retrieved from an authorized reporting source.
- **Scope:** User, device, both, or unknown.
